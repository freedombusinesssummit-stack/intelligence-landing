## 1. Positioning (what we sell)

**Product:** Exclusive, qualified leads for investment-migration firms.
**AI is proof, not product.** The AI qualification layer is the *reason* leads are qualified — it is not the thing we sell. The thing we sell is exclusive qualified leads.

**One-line positioning:**
> AI sorts. Your expert closes.

**Approved framing:**
- Primary noun: "exclusive qualified leads" (never "an AI platform")
- AI is described as a **qualification layer** / **proof of quality**
- Never lead with "AI-Enabled Funnel" as the headline product

## 4. The Process (7-step flow — the key element, kept at top)

The **7-step flow stays at the top of Overview** — it's the core explainer. Retire only the *separate* five-stage deep-dive further down that duplicates it.

| # | Step | What happens |
|---|------|--------------|
| 01 | **We learn your offer** | Discovery call: jurisdiction, programme, client profile |
| 02 | **We define your ICP** | Ideal Client Profile — capital, geography, motivation, timeline |
| 03 | **We run video ads** | Paid video (Meta, Reddit, Nextdoor, YouTube) targets ICP + jurisdiction |
| 04 | **We run the survey** | 14-question Global Mobility Survey; full UTM attribution |
| 05 | **We invite to webinar** | Qualified respondents register for the jurisdiction webinar |

**Retire:** the standalone five-stage "deep dive" (Attract/Capture/Qualify/Score/Deliver) that repeats this flow lower on the page.

## 5. Definitions

**Qualified lead:** A prospect who has (a) opted in, (b) completed the 14-question Global Mobility Survey, and (c) scored 40+ on the Global Mobility Score across 6 dimensions.

**Verified lead:** A qualified lead whose intent has additionally passed the AI qualification layer + intent verification step. This is what lands in the dashboard.

---

## 6. What the partner receives (per lead)

Every delivered lead includes:
1. **Profile** — name, geography, family status
2. **Global Mobility Score** (0–100) + tier (HOT / WARM / COLD)
3. **Advisor Brief** — income, timeline, programme, prospect's own words
4. **Contact details** — unlocked with 1 credit
5. **Consent record** — GDPR opt-in captured at survey
6. **Attribution** — full UTM / source tracking

Plus: **the segmented email list is owned by the partner** and compounds every campaign.

## 7. What volume & speed depend on

- **Media budget** (paid directly by partner to ad platforms)
- **Jurisdiction demand** (inbound vs outbound market)
- **Offer strength** and webinar conversion
- **Follow-up capacity** on the partner's side

No fixed "X leads/month" guarantee is published. Volume scales with budget after the first webinar.
